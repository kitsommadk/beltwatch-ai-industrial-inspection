# Pilot validation plan

## Objective

Determine whether controlled top/bottom imaging can produce repeatable width and
visible-defect evidence on one slitter without interfering with production.

## Stage 1 — Installation and imaging

- Verify mounting clearance and safe cable routing.
- Lock camera distance, angle, exposure, and lighting.
- Confirm the bottom view remains usable in the real environment.
- Record calibration version with each session.

**Exit:** stable, repeatable images across representative belt surfaces.

## Stage 2 — Baseline capture

- Run known-good material at normal operating conditions.
- Quantify width-measurement variation against a trusted manual method.
- Capture surface, color, splice, and edge variation.

**Exit:** documented baseline distribution and labeled reference set.

## Stage 3 — Detection tuning

- Prioritize width deviation, edge irregularity, and visible surface anomalies.
- Review every candidate with the operator.
- Preserve false positives as evaluation examples rather than deleting them.

**Exit:** agreed thresholds and a repeatable evaluation set.

## Stage 4 — Repeatability

- Repeat runs across material types and operating conditions.
- Verify event position, measurement, and evidence linkage.
- Test restart, lost-network, full-storage, and camera-disconnect behavior.

**Exit:** results reproduce across runs and failure modes are understood.

## Stage 5 — Decision

Leadership receives a stop, refine, or advance recommendation based on:

- imaging reliability;
- measurement error;
- detection precision/recall on the agreed evaluation set;
- operator review burden;
- system uptime and recovery; and
- installation/safety findings.

No ROI, accuracy, or production-readiness claim is made before these measurements exist.
